An introduction to KempnerForge, an open-source, PyTorch-native framework for fault-tolerant distributed training of foundation models on shared AI clusters, developed by the Research Engineering Team at the Kempner Institute.

A lightning talk at the Harvard Research Computing and Data (RCD) Summit 2026 in Cambridge, MA.
